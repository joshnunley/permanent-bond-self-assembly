How to read a shape page

Each stock page lists all edge-connected target shapes up to rotation, translation, and reflection. Open a guaranteed target to see all its maximal sets of permitted parts. Intermediate parts keep their proper orientation class, so a reflected part can occupy a separate entry. The factorization count up to reflection identifies a set with its whole mirror image.

A shape listed under “No guaranteeing rule” can still be produced in some histories. How guarantees differ from success probabilities

A smaller part links to the page where it is treated as a target in its own right. A part may occur inside a successful large-stock rule even when it cannot itself be guaranteed at its own stock.
